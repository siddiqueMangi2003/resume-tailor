# Template design references

Resume Tailor's LaTeX is generated by its own deterministic, single-column renderer. Three
optional visual presets adapt general design ideas from templates published in the Overleaf
gallery. They do not bundle the original template classes or sample data.

| Resume Tailor preset | Design reference | Author/maintainer | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alta Teal | [AltaCV](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/altacv-template/trgqjpwnmtgv) | LianTze Lim | LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c |
| Modern Banking | [ModernCV and Cover Letter](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/moderncv-and-cover-letter-template/sttkgjcysttn) | Xavier Danaux and moderncv maintainers | LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c |
| Pagella Scholar | [Minimal LaTeX Resume](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/latex-resume-template/ngdmzkwsbgmd) | Azmain Biswas | LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c |

The preview content is fictional and exists only to demonstrate layout.
